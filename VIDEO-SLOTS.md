Scrolling the 110vh hold, each next clip rises into the window under a straight line; it becomes the current clip (it plays and the caption swaps) once its line passes halfway. Clips get a source only while the chapter is on screen, and play only while it is at the middle of the screen and no dialog is open. On wide screens the seven times are listed beside the window with the current one marked; on narrow screens the caption ("06:12 · 1 / 7", the place, "Neel · No. 08 Ajrakh") shows instead, and the window still runs. "Pause the clips" / "Play the clips" stops or starts them for the visit (sessionStorage); a click, Enter or Space on the window plays or pauses the current clip. Under the window, always visible and never animated: "Illustrative clips made with AI for this concept. The people shown are not customers." With reduced motion (the landing's calm mode) the seven are a sideways row of posters, a caption under each; tapping one plays it alone.

## The opening ("Jal meets Rang", landing L1, rebuilt 20 October 2026): the caustic picture, the unused ink frames, and a video-ready slot for the splash

*JJ ke liye:* hero abhi bhi poora code se chalta hai (koi video nahi). Paani ke andar jo roshni ki jaali (caustics) dikhti hai, woh tumhare apne `v01-water-surface-alt.mp4` se bani ek chhoti tasveer hai (`assets/hero/caustic.webp`). Pehle wale ink ke 48 frames (`assets/hero/ink-*.webp`) ab use nahi hote; woh disk par pade hain, publish aur zip mein nahi jaate. Asli slow-motion splash ke liye abhi credits nahi hain (Higgsfield balance 0.04, 20 October 2026); jab ho, neeche ka prompt chalana.

**What is in the page today.** No video plays in L1. The film runs on real physics and a clock of real time once the first scroll has let the bottle go (`js/landing/hero-phys.js` the physics and the camera, `hero-film.js` the timeline numbers, `hero-fx.js` the water and its layers, `hero-splash.js` the splash, `hero-sprites.js` the droplet and bubble pictures): a tank of red water, the bottle falls, plunges with a drawn splash whose size fits its speed, the camera follows it down and it floats in flat Sindoor water. The water, the splash, the droplets and the bubbles are drawn in code; the caustic light is the one picture made from footage.

**The caustic picture** (`assets/hero/caustic.webp`, a 4 x 4 grey sheet of 256 px tiles, 181 KB, fetched after the page is ready; a procedural stand-in is drawn until it arrives or if it fails). Rebuild it with `python3 -I tools/hero/bake_caustic.py <outdir> [tile px] [quality]`: frames 98 onwards of `footage/v01-water-surface-alt.mp4` (every second frame, 16 frames, 6 cross-faded into a loop); per frame, the luminance minus its own local mean (light only), a gentle curve so the network stays crisp and the floor goes to black; a 120 px band cross-faded so the tile repeats; WebP quality 60.

**The ink frames of the earlier film** (`assets/hero/ink-001.webp` to `ink-048.webp`, from `footage/v02-bloom-c.mp4`, 19 October 2026) are unused since 20 October 2026. They stay on disk (`tools/publish.py` and `tools/package.py` leave them out). The recipe was: ffmpeg to PNG frames, per frame opacity = 1 - L / B (L the luminance, B the empty tank), cropped to the tank, a contrast curve baked in, 48 frames spread over the clip, grey lossy WebP at 800 x 459.

**Video-ready slot: a real slow-motion splash.** If JJ later makes one, it replaces the drawn splash in `hero-splash.js` (and the droplets and bubbles it throws), nothing else. Brief for the clip: side view, locked-off camera, pure black background, RED water only (Sindoor `#C3361D` liquid, lit from behind), **no bottle, no object, no hands**, 16:9 and a 9:16 take, 6 to 8 seconds, no audio, no text. The waterline runs straight across the frame at 82 % of its height (78 % for the 9:16 take) and the splash rises from its middle, so the frame can be laid over the stage with the line on the line.

Name them `l1-splash.mp4` and `l1-splash.webm`, put them in `assets/video/`, add a poster `l1-splash.webp`, and add a row to the "In use" table.
